How it works

Three fields. Then it runs without you.

01

Connect your Instagram

One click. We never see your password — Instagram hands us a token you can revoke whenever you like.

02

Pick a post, set the word

Your real reels and posts are there. Tap one, type the word people should comment, and write the DM they get.

03

Turn it on

From then on every matching comment gets your reply and your link, in seconds, at any hour.

Follow-gate

Make the link worth a follow

Switch it on and the link waits until they follow you. They get a friendly nudge instead, and the moment they follow, the real message goes out.

If Instagram will not tell us whether someone follows you yet, we say so plainly rather than accusing a real follower of not following. That difference is the whole feature.
