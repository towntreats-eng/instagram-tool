Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

Welcome to DMFlow ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide Instagram automation, direct messaging, and comment reply workflows. We are committed to protecting your personal information and privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DMFlow, we only access the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Instagram Profile Information: Username, Account ID, and Page ID linked to Meta Graph API.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to:

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties. Data is only communicated with Meta Platforms, Inc. via their official Graph API to execute your automations.
