User Data Deletion Instructions

DMFlow values your privacy and data sovereignty. If you wish to delete your data or revoke app permissions from Facebook and Instagram, please follow the steps below:

How to Remove DMFlow from your Facebook / Instagram Account:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account's Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings.
  2. Scroll down to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find DMFlow in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove to revoke all access and permissions.
  5. Optionally check the box: "Delete posts, videos or events that DMFlow posted on your timeline".

How to Request Full Data Deletion from DMFlow:

To request permanent deletion of all your stored data (including campaign logs, contacts, and configuration tokens) from our database:

  • Send an email to umangptl11@gmail.com with the subject: "Data Deletion Request".
  • Include your connected Instagram handle / Facebook Page name.
  • Our team will permanently purge all your records within 48 hours and send you a confirmation email.
