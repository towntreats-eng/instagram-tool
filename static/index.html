ConverFlow Documentation & Setup Guide

How does ConverFlow work?
ConverFlow monitors your Instagram Reel comments in real-time. When a follower comments a target keyword (e.g. "link"), ConverFlow publicly replies with human-like spintax to boost algorithmic reach, and simultaneously delivers your resources directly to their DMs!
1. How do I connect my Instagram Account?

Go to Settings and press Connect Instagram. One click, no tokens to copy — you just need a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook page.

2. How does the 15-Day Free Trial work?

Every workspace starts on a 15-day trial of our recommended plan. After that you pick a plan that fits — prices are flat, and contacts are included rather than billed per head, so a viral reel never changes your bill.

3. Why does an Opening DM with a button convert better?

Instagram's algorithm favors two-way interactions. When users click an interactive button inside DMs, Instagram flags your account as a high-engagement creator, boosting your reel's ranking on the Explore page!
