Data Deletion Instructions

Service: DM Flow Company: Satnam Web Services, Unjha, Gujarat, India Compliance: Meta Platform Terms & GDPR Article 17

At DM Flow, we respect your data sovereignty and privacy. In compliance with Meta's Platform Terms, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), you have the full legal right to request the permanent deletion of any data associated with your Facebook / Instagram account or DM Flow workspace.

What Happens When You Request Data Deletion?

All stored Instagram tokens, linked profile identifiers, conversation interaction records, keyword trigger logs, and contact history are permanently removed from our production databases and backup queues.

Option 1: Disconnect & Remove DM Flow via Meta (Recommended)

You can revoke all DM Flow access directly from your Facebook or Instagram security settings in under 60 seconds:

Through Facebook Desktop / Mobile:

  1. Log into your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate DM Flow in the active applications list.
  4. Click Remove.
  5. Check the confirmation box: "Delete posts, videos or events that DM Flow posted on your timeline" and confirm.

Through Instagram Mobile App:

  1. Open Instagram and tap your Profile icon > Menu (top right) > Settings and privacy.
  2. Scroll down to Website permissions and select Apps and websites.
  3. Tap the Active tab, locate DM Flow, and tap Remove.

Option 2: Direct Support Request (48-Hour SLA)

If you would like our engineering team to completely wipe all historical logs, campaign metrics, or customer contact records associated with your email or Instagram account:
